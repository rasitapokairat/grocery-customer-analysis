# Grocery Customer Analysis

#### Tasks: 
- Two insights will need to be extracted from [Tesco Grocery 1.0 Dataset](https://figshare.com/collections/Tesco_Grocery_1_0/4769354) and visualisations will be created to represent them. First research question is "How gender and age impact to purchased product category?" and second reseach question is "How different in each category purchases based on areas with high and low purchases of fruit and vegetable?"
- Extract insight from income data: [Regional gross disposable household income: local authorities by ITL1 region](https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/regionalaccounts/grossdisposablehouseholdincome/datasets/regionalgrossdisposablehouseholdincomelocalauthoritiesbyitl1region) and visualize it.
- Extract correlation or non-correlation from both dataset by referencing the finding from [US Adults' Fruit and Vegetable Intakes,1989 to 1991: A Revised Baseline for the Healthy People 2000 Objective](https://ajph.aphapublications.org/doi/pdf/10.2105/AJPH.85.12.1623).
